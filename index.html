02 — Who you will meet

Five people who get the week you just had.

Six seats. Here is how we fill the other five.

Seat 01

A stage you recognise

Roughly your altitude — pre-revenue with pre-revenue, post-Series-A with post-Series-A. Advice only lands between people carrying similar weight.

Seat 02

A different industry

Never a direct competitor. You can say the real number out loud because nobody at the table is going to use it.

Seat 03

Someone further along

One seat is held for a founder who has already been where the table is heading. Not a mentor, not billing you. Just further down the same road.

Seat 04

Someone who disagrees with you

The questionnaire measures how you handle being wrong. We deliberately seat one person whose answers cut against yours.

Seat 05

Nobody from your circle

Same cap table, same accelerator batch, same phone contacts — all filtered out. You will not be seated with someone you would have met anyway.

Seat 06

You

Bring nothing. No deck, no one-liner, no version of yourself that sounds fundable. The quiz already did the introduction.

04 — Trust and safety

Say yes without checking anyone out first.

The things you would want to know before eating with five strangers, in plain words.

Verified by a person

Every member shows a government ID and one proof they are building what they say — a domain, a GST number, an app listing. A human checks both.

The table stays at the table

No recording, no posting, no adding people to a CRM afterwards. Everyone agrees to it at signup; breaking it ends the membership.

Venues we have eaten in

Well lit, reachable, quiet enough to hear the far end of the table. We have met the manager and sat at the exact table you will get.

No investors in disguise

Nobody is seated to source deals. If a fund wants in, they host their own table under their own name, announced up front.

07 — Questions

What founders ask first.

Who counts as a founder?
Anyone currently building something they own a piece of — registered or not, funded or not, full time or nights and weekends. Solo founders, co-founders and early founding-team employees sit at the same tables. What we screen for is that you are carrying real risk, not a job title.
Is this a networking event?
No. There is no stage, no name tags, no round of introductions and no organiser standing up to talk. It is six people having dinner. Whatever comes out of it is between you.
I am early and unfunded. Will I be out of place?
You will be seated with people at your altitude. A table where one person has raised a Series B and another is on week three is bad for both of them, so we do not build it.
Who pays for the dinner?
You do, at the restaurant, at menu price. Membership covers matching, venue work and the reservation — never a markup on your food.
What if I cannot make a week?
Decline the invite. Nothing is charged per dinner, so skipping one costs you nothing but that seat. The month covers the rest.
Can I bring my co-founder?
Not to your table. They are welcome to join and take the quiz, and we will make sure you are never seated together — two people from the same company changes how everyone else talks.
What if you cannot seat me?
Then you should not be paying. If we have not seated you at a table within your first 30 days, we refund the month in full. Tell us on WhatsApp; there is no form.
Which cities?
Delhi NCR first — South Delhi, Gurgaon and Noida. Bengaluru and Mumbai open next, and the quiz lets you mark interest so we know where to go.
